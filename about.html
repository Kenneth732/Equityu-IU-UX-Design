More Than A Bank, A Movement

For over three decades, Equity Bank has been transforming lives and building prosperity across Africa. We're not just a financial institution—we're a catalyst for economic growth, innovation, and community development.

Our Journey

Founded in 1984 as a building society, Equity Bank began with a simple mission: to provide financial services to those who had been excluded from the formal banking sector. What started as a small institution serving Kenya's low-income population has grown into a pan-African financial services group serving millions of customers across six countries.

Our transformation from a struggling building society to a leading financial institution is a story of resilience, innovation, and unwavering commitment to our customers. We pioneered agency banking in East Africa, launched mobile banking solutions that reached the unbanked, and consistently put our customers at the center of everything we do.

Today, we're proud to be listed on multiple stock exchanges, recognized internationally for our innovative approach to banking, and most importantly, trusted by millions of Africans to help them build better futures.
