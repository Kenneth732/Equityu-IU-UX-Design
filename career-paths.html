Typical Career Journey at Equity Bank

Years 1-2

Foundation Building

Complete comprehensive training programs, rotate through different departments, and build fundamental banking skills. Most employees start in entry-level positions like Teller or Junior Analyst.

  • Comprehensive onboarding program
  • Department rotations
  • Basic certification programs
  • Mentorship assignment
Years 3-5

Specialization & Growth

Develop expertise in your chosen field, take on more responsibilities, and contribute to key projects. Many employees move into specialist or supervisory roles during this phase.

  • Advanced certification programs
  • Project leadership opportunities
  • Cross-functional team participation
  • Performance-based promotions
Years 6-8

Leadership Development

Prepare for management positions through leadership training programs, strategic projects, and mentorship of junior staff. This phase focuses on developing strategic thinking and people management skills.

  • Leadership development program
  • Strategic initiative participation
  • Team management experience
  • Executive mentorship
Years 8+

Strategic Leadership

Take on executive or senior management roles, drive business strategy, and contribute to organizational direction. Focus on innovation, business development, and talent development.

  • Executive leadership program
  • Board committee participation
  • Industry thought leadership
  • Succession planning involvement
